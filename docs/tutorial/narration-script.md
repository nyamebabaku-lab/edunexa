# EduNexa guided tour — narration script

Video: `EduNexa-Tutorial.mp4` · length 13:38 · 89 lines

Read each line when its time comes up. The same words appear as captions in the video, so the voice should match them closely; small natural changes are fine. A warm, friendly, unhurried tone works best — like a head teacher showing a visitor around the school.

## Welcome  `0:00`

_Title card — music or a short welcome, e.g. "Akwaaba to EduNexa."_


## 1. Signing in  `0:07`

- **0:10** Akwaaba! Welcome to EduNexa, the complete management system for Bright Future International School — from Creche all the way to Diploma.
- **0:19** Everyone signs in to their own portal: administrators, teachers, parents, students, the accountant, librarian, storekeeper, transport officer, canteen manager and support staff.
- **0:28** In demo mode you can open any portal with one click. Each portal shows only what that person is allowed to see.
- **0:37** A school can switch on secure sign-in, with a password and a verification code sent to the phone.
- **0:45** Staff use their email or phone, students use their admission number, and parents can create their own account.
- **0:53** Let's go back and open the Administrator portal.

## 2. Admin dashboard  `1:01`

- **1:04** This is the head's dashboard. At a glance: enrolment, staff, fees collected this term, and fees still outstanding.
- **1:12** The Needs attention panel flags what matters today — gate arrivals, low stock, overdue library books and pending leave.
- **1:21** Charts show income against expenditure, enrolment by section and the attendance trend.
- **1:27** The menu on the left groups every module. On a phone it becomes a bottom bar.
- **1:35** Search finds any student, staff member or page instantly. Press Control K from anywhere.
- **1:42** And there is a dark mode for evening work.

## 3. Admissions  `1:53`

- **1:58** Admissions tracks every applicant from application, to entrance exam, interview, offer and enrolment.
- **2:04** For an applicant who has been offered a place, you can print a formal admission letter with the fees to pay.
- **2:13** One click on Enrol creates the student, the parent record and the first fee bill together.
- **2:23** And here is the new student, ready in the system.

## 4. Students & photos  `2:28`

- **2:34** The Students page lists every learner. Filter by section, class or status, or export to Excel.
- **2:41** Let's open Kofi Mensah in JHS 2.
- **2:51** His profile brings everything together: personal details, parent contacts, medical notes, results, attendance, fees, library and conduct.
- **2:59** To add a photo, tap the camera button. You can upload a picture or take one with the camera.
- **3:07** Drag and zoom to frame the face, then save.
- **3:16** The photo now appears on his ID card, report card, gate badge and everywhere he is listed.
- **3:24** The tabs show his academic results, attendance record and fee statement.

## 5. Staff, HR & payroll  `3:33`

- **3:38** Staff and HR keeps every teacher and worker, with roles, qualifications, leave requests and approvals.
- **3:44** Each staff member has a profile with their teaching load, pay and a printable staff ID card.
- **3:54** Payroll calculates salaries every month with Ghana SSNIT and PAYE deductions, ready for the bank.
- **4:01** Click any line to print a payslip.

## 6. Classes & timetable  `4:06`

- **4:10** Classes are organised by section: Early Years, Primary, JHS, SHS programmes and Diploma.
- **4:16** Open a class to see its subjects, the teacher for each one, and the students.
- **4:24** The timetable shows the whole week for any class, with breaks and lunch. The administrator can edit it here.

## 7. Attendance register  `4:33`

- **4:37** Teachers take the daily register in seconds. Mark everyone present with one tap…
- **4:43** …then change anyone who is late or absent.
- **4:47** Save the register, and with one more tap, parents of absent children receive a text message.

## 8. Gradebook & report cards  `4:56`

- **4:59** In the gradebook, teachers enter class scores out of thirty and exam scores out of seventy.
- **5:06** Totals, grades and positions are calculated automatically, using the GES, WASSCE or Diploma grading scale.
- **5:17** Report cards are generated for the whole class, ranked, with remarks and the BECE aggregate for JHS.
- **5:24** Open one to print it, or print the whole class at once.

## 9. Learning (LMS)  `5:31`

- **5:34** The learning platform lets teachers post lesson notes, slides, worksheets and video links for each class.
- **5:41** Assignments show who has submitted and what still needs marking.
- **5:47** And quizzes mark themselves the moment a student submits.

## 10. Fees & finance  `5:53`

- **5:55** This is the accountant's portal: money billed and collected this term, arrears and expenses.
- **6:03** Fees and Finance holds every invoice, payment, arrears list, fee structure and expense.
- **6:09** Recording a payment takes seconds — Mobile Money, cash, bank transfer or cheque.
- **6:17** Save it, and an official receipt is ready to print or share.
- **6:24** The Arrears tab lists everyone who owes, with one-tap SMS reminders to parents.

## 11. Canteen  `6:32`

- **6:36** The canteen manager sells food from a simple point of sale — jollof, waakye, banku and tilapia, kelewele, sobolo and more.
- **6:45** Tap items to add them to the order…
- **6:54** …choose the customer and payment — cash, MoMo, or add it to the student's fee bill — and complete the sale.
- **7:03** Plan the weekly menu, track kitchen stock, and watch daily income and best sellers.

## 12. Store, library, transport & more  `7:11`

- **7:15** The store tracks uniforms, books and supplies, with low-stock alerts and purchase orders.
- **7:22** The library manages the catalogue, loans and returns, and works out overdue fines automatically.
- **7:29** Transport manages buses, routes, drivers and riders, trips and maintenance.
- **7:35** For boarding schools: halls, bed allocation and exeats.
- **7:41** And health and discipline records keep every infirmary visit and incident on file.

## 13. QR gate attendance  `7:48`

- **7:53** Gate Attendance shows who has arrived this morning, who is late, and who has not come in yet — for students and staff.
- **8:03** At the entrance, a tablet runs the gate kiosk.
- **8:07** It shows a QR code that changes every thirty seconds. Students and staff scan it with their phone to clock in and out.
- **8:17** Pupils without phones hold up their ID badge to the kiosk camera. Their photo appears so the security officer can confirm it is really them — and the parent gets a text.

## 14. Teacher portal & clock-in  `8:37`

- **8:39** This is Mr. Kwame Asante's portal: his lessons for today, work to mark, and a reminder to take the register.
- **8:48** He clocks in by scanning the gate code — or typing the six-digit code shown on the kiosk.
- **8:55** His arrival time is recorded, and late arrivals are flagged automatically.

## 15. Live classes  `9:02`

- **9:05** Teachers can teach live, with camera, microphone, screen sharing and chat. Each class has its own code and invite link.
- **9:14** Before starting, check the camera and microphone, and choose low-data or audio-only mode for slow connections.
- **9:26** Students join with the class code or invite link. Here, Kofi has just joined from his phone.
- **9:33** Students can raise a hand, chat, and turn their camera or microphone off at any time.
- **9:41** The teacher can mute everyone, lock microphones or cameras, remove a student, and end the class for all. Attendance is saved automatically.

## 16. Parent portal  `9:54`

- **9:58** Mrs. Akosua Mensah has two children in the school. Her portal shows each child's attendance, average and fees owing.
- **10:07** She can see their results for every term and download the report card.
- **10:14** And she can pay school fees with Mobile Money, right from her phone.
- **10:33** She can also check the school bus route and stops, and the canteen menu.

## 17. Student portal  `10:40`

- **10:43** Kofi's own portal shows today's lessons, homework due, quizzes and his results.
- **10:50** He can take a quiz on his phone, with a timer, and see his score straight away.
- **11:16** He can browse the library catalogue and check his borrowed books.

## 18. Staff portals  `11:22`

- **11:24** Every staff member gets a portal built for their job. The librarian sees overdue books and fines.
- **11:33** The storekeeper sees stock value and the reorder list.
- **11:39** The transport officer sees every route, bus and rider.
- **11:45** And support staff — drivers, security, cleaners and cooks — can clock in and read school notices.

## 19. Super Admin settings  `11:53`

- **11:57** The Super Admin sets up the system for each school: name, logo and colours.
- **12:03** Choose a colour theme, and the whole app changes instantly.
- **12:11** Modules a school does not use — like hostel or canteen — can be switched off.
- **12:18** Login and security controls password rules, verification codes, parent sign-up and account lockout.
- **12:24** And the Users tab approves new parent accounts and resets passwords.

## 20. Works offline  `12:29`

- **12:34** EduNexa keeps working even when the internet goes off.
- **12:40** The orange bar shows we are offline — but every page still opens, and all your work is saved on the device.
- **12:51** Text messages sent while offline wait in a queue…
- **13:01** …and go out automatically as soon as the connection returns.

## 21. On your phone  `13:07`

- **13:10** On a phone, EduNexa looks and feels like an app, with a bottom menu.
- **13:17** Install it: on iPhone, Safari, Share, Add to Home Screen. On Android, Chrome menu, Install app.
- **13:24** Then it opens straight from the home screen — even without data.

## Thank you  `13:31`

_Closing card — "Medaase for watching. Sign in, pick a portal and explore."_
